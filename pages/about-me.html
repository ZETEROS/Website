ABOUT ME

My name is Mohamed Zahrani. I’m 21 years old and have been passionate about computer science since I was 7.

I started out simply curious about Wi-Fi, and the more time I spent on it, the more I delved into the field of cybersecurity. Like many others who share my passion, I was always fascinated by how hackers were portrayed in movies and TV shows, it always seemed really cool to me. Now that I’ve gotten into cybersecurity myself, I’ve discovered it’s not as simple as saying, “I’m in!” But even so, I find it super interesting and I’m still learning on my own. I’m currently studying at CY Tech in Cergy. I’ve gained a more in-depth understanding of various programming languages like C and Bash (useful for Linux terminals), as well as web development, which allowed me to create this very portfolio. I’m currently continuing to practice and improve my hacking techniques thanks to sites like HackTheBox and TryHackMe.

In the “Write-Ups” section of my portfolio, you’ll find the various write-ups for different machines I’ve hacked on the websites mentioned above. Due to the usage policies of these sites, I’m only allowed to publish machines classified as “Retired,” so the number of machines listed in the “SOME STATS” section on the Home page may not match the number of write-ups. I’ll try to keep publishing my write-ups and hope you find them interesting. In the “Projects” section, I’ll post brief explanations and direct links to my real-world projects.
